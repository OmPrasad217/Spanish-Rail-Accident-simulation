Scene overview

A Castilian-La Mancha-inspired landscape frames a schematic double-track corridor. Terrain, route spacing and landmarks are illustrative, not surveyed coordinates.

Verified record & modelling limits

AITAT's first-day note identifies Iryo 6076 (Hitachi ETR1000 109.015) and Renfe AVE 2080 (Siemens 103.006 variant), both eight-car sets. It reports Iryo stopped at entry signal E242 when the Renfe train struck it from behind.

The first-day note says the investigation was at an early evidence-gathering stage and left possible causes open. This simulation assigns no cause and asserts no speed, approach time or physical scale.

The high-detail procedural train models include smoothed aerodynamic cabs, reflective painted shells, framed glazing, doors, roof equipment, pantographs, gangways, detailed bogies and rolling wheel profiles; they are still visual interpretations, not photogrammetry. Track position, countryside, signal marker, spacing and post-impact arrangement are schematic; the final tableau is not an exact damage map.
